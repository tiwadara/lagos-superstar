# 0011. Brand: a multi-career name, Nollywood poster style, danfo as an accent

- **Status:** Proposed. Needs a decision from the owner
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Issue:** #47

## Context

The game is moving beyond music (#39), and the title "The Next Lagos Star" fits any career. The first playtester didn't like the yellow, though they understood it was the danfo (#13). The owner has floated "Nollywood" and "naija superstars" as directions. A comparable music game exists, so the brand has to say "the whole industry", not "a musician".

## Options considered

1. **Keep "The Next Lagos Star", restyle it.** No name change, and it already fits every career.
2. **An umbrella brand, "Naija Superstars", with "The Next Lagos Star" as the first game.** Room for other cities later, but two names to explain.
3. **Visual directions:** a Nollywood poster style (hand-painted, saturated, dramatic faces), owambe glam (aso-ebi, gold), Lagos after dark (stage lights), or danfo, quieter (the stripe as an accent on a calmer base).

## Recommendation

- Keep the name **The Next Lagos Star**. It already covers every career and is already live and shared.
- Use the **Nollywood poster** style for headings, endings and the share card. It fits a game about fame across music, film and the rest.
- Keep the **danfo** as an accent only: the stripe and the slogans. Drop the full yellow panel.

## Decision

To be made by the owner. Mock-ups of the two strongest directions, shown to three or more playtesters, should settle it (#47).

## Consequences

- The design system (#48), animations (#49), icon and link preview (#50), and portraits (#30, ADR 0012) follow from this choice.
