# 0007. All people and companies are fictional

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-05

## Context

The game satirises the Lagos entertainment industry: labels, blogs, investors, politicians, police and brands. Naming real people or companies in that light could be unfair or defamatory, and would date the game quickly.

## Decision

We will invent every person, company, brand, label, blog, station and investor in the game. Real places, foods, public services and everyday things of Lagos life are welcome. We will not name or clearly imitate a real artist, public figure or company.

## Consequences

- Writers can be pointed about the industry without targeting anyone.
- Every new name must be checked against real ones before it ships. The [story-writing guide](../story-writing-guide.md#fiction-rule) includes this in its checklist.
- The README states that all characters, companies and events are fictional. The game itself does not say so yet. Adding the line to the title screen is tracked in the launch epic.
